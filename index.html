      About Mohidoi

      Mohidoi started in 2023 as a ひどい (“terrible”) little page for learning hiragana and katakana. Version 2 adds real recordings of everyday words, a searchable word list and short practice sessions that bring back the things you miss.

      How practice works

      Every question has four answers. Items you get wrong come back later in the same session and turn up more often in later sessions; items you keep getting right show up less. Your progress is kept only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
