All recordings in this folder are by the Wikimedia Commons / Lingua Libre user Fukujinzuke
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/User:Fukujinzuke), recorded with Lingua Libre (https://lingualibre.org/),
licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Source files: "LL-Q5287 (jpn)-Fukujinzuke-<word>.wav" on commons.wikimedia.org.

Kana clips (audio/<hiragana>.wav)
  Files renamed to <hiragana>.wav; audio unchanged.
  に uses the speaker's katakana ニ recording (same sound).

Word clips (audio/words/<id>.ogg)
  Every Japanese recording by Fukujinzuke on Commons (1,358 files, listed in October 2026).
  Changes: converted from WAV to mono Opus (Ogg container, ~28 kbps, 48 kHz) with ffmpeg,
  leading/trailing silence trimmed, metadata removed. <id> is the first 10 hex digits of the
  SHA-1 of the Commons file name; data/words.js maps each id back to the recorded word, so the
  original file is "LL-Q5287 (jpn)-Fukujinzuke-<word>.wav".

Dictionary data (data/words.js)
  Readings, meanings and parts of speech come from JMdict, (c) the Electronic Dictionary Research
